# observation-statistics

First determine the observation unit, population, and estimation target; distinguish individuals, culture units, batches, and replicate measurements. Explain the impact of sampling, missingness, and multiple comparisons on the scope of understanding. Descriptive atlases can contribute distributions, heterogeneity, or resource capabilities, without forcibly recasting them as mechanistic proof. Provide the value and uncertainty of different possible observations.
